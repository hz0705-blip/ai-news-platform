Negotiators from Caldera and the Vantage Federation wrapped up talks in Thornholt on Tuesday, ending a long-running dispute over fishing grounds. The agreement was signed in Thornholt on Tuesday. Voss said the agreement ends years of disputes over shared waters. The pact sets new quotas and creates a joint patrol board for the disputed waters. A joint monitoring system is expected to begin operating early next year, officials said. Ratification by both parliaments is required before the framework takes effect. Fishing communities on both sides of the border welcomed the news after years of uncertainty. Regional observers called the pact a rare diplomatic success story.
