Kaldera's parliament on Wednesday approved a bill that hands operation of the Port Kessel container terminal to a private concession for 25 years. Lawmakers passed the measure by 88 votes to 61 after a debate that ran past midnight.

The concession will be awarded through an open tender that the transport ministry said it would launch within three months. The winning operator must keep the terminal's current workforce of about 1,400 employees for at least five years, according to the text of the bill.

The dockworkers' union said it would study the final text before deciding whether to challenge the law in court. Shipping lines that use the port have said for several years that the terminal needs new cranes and deeper berths to handle the larger vessels now common on Asia routes.
