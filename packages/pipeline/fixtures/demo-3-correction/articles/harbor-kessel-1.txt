Kaldera's parliament on Wednesday postponed a vote on a bill that would hand operation of the Port Kessel container terminal to a private concession for 25 years, after opposition lawmakers requested more time to review amendments.

The concession would be awarded through an open tender run by the transport ministry. Under the bill, the winning operator would have to keep the terminal's current workforce of about 1,400 employees for at least five years.

The dockworkers' union has said it opposes any concession that does not guarantee existing pension terms. Shipping lines that use the port have long complained about crane breakdowns and shallow berths.
