A strike by dockworkers at the Beryl Port container terminal began on Tuesday after wage negotiations broke down. The union represents most of the terminal's crane operators and lashing crews.

Two of the terminal's four berths continued handling containers on Tuesday, according to the port authority's operations log. Throughput was well below normal, and trucking companies reported queues at the gate through the afternoon.

Both sides are expected to return to the negotiating table on Thursday. Shippers said they were watching for any sign that the stoppage would spread to the port's bulk terminal.
