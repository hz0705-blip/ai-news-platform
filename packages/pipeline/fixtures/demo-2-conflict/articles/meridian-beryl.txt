Dockworkers at the Beryl Port container terminal began a strike on Tuesday after wage talks collapsed. The walkout followed three weeks of negotiations between the dockworkers' union and the Beryl Port Authority over a new two-year pay agreement.

The terminal suspended all container operations on Tuesday, the port authority said. Ships already berthed remained alongside, and vessels scheduled to arrive later in the week were told to expect delays.

The union and the port authority are expected to resume talks on Thursday. Neither side gave details of the outstanding differences, and the authority did not say how long the suspension would last.
