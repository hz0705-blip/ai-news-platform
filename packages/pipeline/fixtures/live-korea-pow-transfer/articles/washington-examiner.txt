A similar controversy helped bring down an earlier government and left a lasting backlash that eventually ended in criminal convictions for senior officials.
In 2021, two fishermen were picked up by Aldmark’s coast guard off the northern shore. Then-President Corvin Hale and his government described the men as “violent offenders” and “a threat to the public” after investigators concluded they had killed several crewmates during an uprising against their skipper before crossing the sea boundary. They were sent back to Veskar.
Veskar defectors who are returned to their country almost always face prison, torture or execution.
Human rights groups denounced the fishermen’s return as a de facto death sentence, while opposition politicians portrayed the handover as Serin giving in to Doravik.
The following government, led by since-impeached President Idris Varn, reopened the investigation in 2024. The Serin Central District Court convicted four former officials over the repatriation orders, though every one of them received a suspended sentence.
